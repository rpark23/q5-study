## Anticoagulants

### Warfarin (Coumadin)
- **MOA:** Inhibits vitamin K epoxide reductase → decreased factors II, VII, IX, X, protein C/S
- **Route:** PO
- **Monitoring:** PT/INR
- **Key points:**
  - Must initiate with heparin bridge to prevent early hypercoagulability (protein C/S have shorter half-lives than procoagulant factors)
  - **Reversal:** Vitamin K

### Unfractionated Heparin (UFH)
- **MOA:** Activates antithrombin → inhibits factors IIa (thrombin) AND Xa
- **Route:** IV, SQ
- **Monitoring:** PTT and anti-Xa level
- **Key points:**
  - Risk for heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT)
  - **Reversal:** Protamine

### Low Molecular Weight Heparin (LMWH): Enoxaparin (Lovenox), Dalteparin
- **MOA:** Activates antithrombin → primarily inhibits factor Xa
- **Route:** SQ
- **Monitoring:** PTT and anti-Xa level
- **Reversal:** Protamine

### Key Differences: UFH vs LMWH
| Feature | UFH | LMWH |
|---------|-----|------|
| Target | IIa and Xa | Primarily Xa |
| Route | IV or SQ | SQ only |
| Monitoring | PTT, anti-Xa | Anti-Xa |
| HIT risk | Higher | Lower |
| Reversal | Protamine (full) | Protamine (partial) |

### Fondaparinux
- **MOA:** Activates antithrombin → selectively inhibits factor Xa
- **Route:** SQ

### Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs): Apixaban (Eliquis), Rivaroxaban (Xarelto)
- **MOA:** Direct factor Xa inhibitor (anti-Xa)
- **Route:** PO
- **Key points:**
  - Reversal agent andexanet no longer available

### Direct Thrombin Inhibitors
- **Lepirudin, Bivalirudin, Argatroban:** IV administration
- **Dabigatran:** PO administration
- **Reversal:** Dabigatran reversed with **idarucizumab**

## Antiplatelet Agents

### Aspirin (ASA)
- **MOA:** Irreversible COX-1/2 inhibition → decreased TXA₂ production
- **Route:** PO

### ADP Receptor Antagonists (P2Y₁₂ Inhibitors)
- **Drugs:** Clopidogrel (Plavix), Ticlopidine, Prasugrel, Ticagrelor (Brilinta)
- **MOA:** Block ADP (P2Y₁₂) receptor on platelets
- **Route:** PO
- Note: Clopidogrel and prasugrel are prodrugs requiring active metabolites; ticagrelor and cangrelor are direct-acting

### GPIIb/IIIa Receptor Antagonists
- **Drugs:** Abciximab (ReoPro), Eptifibatide (Integrilin)
- **MOA:** Block the GPIIb/IIIa receptor (final common pathway of platelet aggregation)
- **Route:** IV

### Dipyridamole (Persantine)
- **MOA:** PDE inhibitor → increased cAMP → inhibits platelet activation
- **Route:** PO

---

## Thrombolytic Agents

- **Drugs:** Tissue plasminogen activator (tPA), Streptokinase, Urokinase
- **MOA:** Convert plasminogen to plasmin → plasmin degrades fibrin clots
- **Clinical use:** Acute myocardial infarction, acute ischemic stroke, massive pulmonary embolism
